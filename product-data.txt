# Northwind Devices - Internal Product Knowledge Base
# Last updated: 2025-03-14
# Audience: support engineers, solutions team

## ND-100 Edge Gateway

The ND-100 is our entry-level industrial edge gateway. It ships with 4 GB RAM,
a quad-core ARM Cortex-A53, and two gigabit Ethernet ports. It supports Modbus
TCP and MQTT out of the box. Maximum sustained throughput is 800 messages per
second. Operating temperature range is -20C to 60C. The ND-100 does not include
onboard TPM, so it is not approved for deployments that require hardware-backed
attestation. List price is 480 USD. Standard warranty is 24 months.

## ND-200 Edge Gateway

The ND-200 is the successor to the ND-100. It has 8 GB RAM, a six-core CPU, and
adds an onboard TPM 2.0 module, which makes it suitable for regulated
deployments. Maximum sustained throughput is 2,400 messages per second, roughly
three times the ND-100. It supports Modbus TCP, MQTT, and OPC-UA. Operating
temperature range is -40C to 70C. List price is 910 USD. Standard warranty is
36 months. The ND-200 is pin-compatible with ND-100 mounting brackets, so field
upgrades do not require new enclosures.

## Firmware and update policy

All gateways run NorthwindOS, a Yocto-based Linux distribution. Firmware updates
are delivered as signed A/B images, so a failed update rolls back automatically
on next boot. Security patches are published on the first Tuesday of each month.
The ND-100 receives security patches until December 2026. The ND-200 receives
security patches until December 2029. Customers on the Extended Support plan get
an additional 24 months beyond those dates.

## Licensing

The base firmware licence is perpetual and included in the hardware price. The
OPC-UA protocol pack is a separate subscription at 120 USD per device per year,
and is only available on the ND-200. The Fleet Manager cloud console is licensed
per site: 1,200 USD per year for up to 50 devices, 4,000 USD per year for up to
500 devices. There is no free tier, but a 30-day evaluation licence is available
on request.

## Support tiers

Standard support covers email response within one business day, 09:00-17:00 CET.
Priority support covers 24/7 phone and email with a four-hour response target,
and costs 18 percent of hardware list price annually. Priority support is
required for any customer running more than 200 devices in production. On-site
hardware replacement is available in the EU and North America only, with a
next-business-day target.

## Known issues

Under firmware 4.2.1, ND-200 units running OPC-UA and MQTT simultaneously can
exhaust file descriptors after approximately 14 days of uptime, causing new
connections to be refused. The workaround is to raise the descriptor limit to
16384 or schedule a weekly service restart. This is fixed in firmware 4.2.3.
ND-100 units are not affected because they do not support OPC-UA.

## Return policy

Unopened hardware may be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Opened
hardware carries a 15 percent restocking fee. Units that have had firmware
downgraded below 4.0 are not eligible for return, because the downgrade path
does not preserve the secure-boot key slots.
